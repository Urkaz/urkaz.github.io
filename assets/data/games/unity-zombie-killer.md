# Overview

Developed in Unity over the course of one week as the final assignment for the "Unity" subject in the "Master's degree in Videogame Programming" at U-Tad.

Unity Zombie Killer is a first-person shooter set in dark, abandoned environments filled with zombies. The player can choose between three types of weapons and has the ability to crouch, jump, and sprint. To escape and survive, the player must locate and collect three key items hidden across the map, eliminating as many zombies as possible along the way.

# My role

I was responsible for programming the enemy AI and implementing the quest system.

## Challenges

### Enemy AI

One of the assignment's requirements was to create the enemy AI from scratch. I took the opportunity to apply what I had learned in the "Artificial Intelligence" subject by implementing a simple but functional state machine in C#.

The state machine featured five states: Idle, Wandering, Chasing, Attacking, and Dead. Enemies could also react to sounds generated by weapons and to what they could see in their field of view.

### Quest system

I implemented the quest system in just a few hours. It consisted of a small list of handcrafted quests, each linked to specific game objects. When a quest was selected, its associated targets, and a few guiding lights, became visible on the map.

# Game Description

## A Massive map with objectives!

The large open map includes a variety of areas, such as a lake, an urban zone, and desolate fields. These areas feature multiple explorable buildings. In each mission, the player must collect three specific items scattered across the environment to complete the objective.

## Different types of weapons and pickups!

The player has access to three weapon types: a pistol, a submachine gun, and a shotgun. Each weapon uses different types of ammo, which can run out quickly. Health and ammo pickups are spread across the map to help the player stay alive.

## Enemies with various types of detection!

The zombies' AI incorporates both sound and vision-based detection. If the player makes noise nearby, like firing a weapon, enemies will instantly become aware of their location and give chase immediately!
